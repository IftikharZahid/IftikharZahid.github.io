Enrollment Pipeline

A simple 4-step
admissions process

1

Online Inquiry

Fill out our digital inquiry form on the contact page or via our mobile app. Our administration will contact you with an appointment schedule for visitation.

2

Campus Visit & Counseling

Visit the academy campus in Fort Abbas to discuss subject combinations (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS) with our academic counselors.

3

Assessment Interview

Candidates will undergo a very brief diagnostic interview to assess basic aptitude and determine the academic focus area for matriculation or intermediate studies.

4

App Onboarding

Once enrolled, you'll immediately receive credentials for The Seeks Academy Mobile App and Web Portal, granting instant access to timetables, digital notices, and your student dashboard.
