Platform Features

Everything a student needs,
in one place

Built specifically for The Seeks Academy — purpose-built academic management for Fort Abbas students, not an off-the-shelf template.

Student Dashboard

A clean, personalised home screen with quick access to all modules — greets students by name with real-time data.

Attendance Tracking

Automated attendance records with detailed history. Parents and students can monitor presence at any time.

Results & Exams

Publish and view exam results instantly. Detailed subject-wise breakdowns with performance trends over time.

Timetable Management

Digital class timetables for every section. Changes sync instantly so students always have the correct schedule.

Fee Management

Track monthly fee status, outstanding dues, and payment confirmations — with full admin control from the dashboard.

Notices & Messaging

Broadcast official notices and enable direct teacher–student communication within the secure app environment.

Student Portal

Designed for
daily academic use

Students open the app and immediately see what matters: their schedule, latest notices, course videos, and quick action shortcuts — all in a clean, distraction-free interface.

  • Quick Actions Grid One-tap access to Assignments, Teachers, Results, Timetable, Attendance, Fees, Notices and Help.
  • Course Video Library Watch and revisit lesson recordings organised by subject and class section.
  • Personalised Greeting Context-aware home screen showing the student's name, time of day, and latest updates.
