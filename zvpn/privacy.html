1. Introduction

ZVPN ("we", "our", or "the app") provides VPN connectivity features for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use ZVPN.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in the released version, ZVPN may process technical information needed to provide VPN connectivity, such as server configuration data, connection status, application diagnostics, and device/network information required for the VPN service to function.

3. VPN Traffic

A VPN service routes network traffic through a VPN connection. The exact handling, retention, and logging of VPN traffic depends on the technical configuration of the ZVPN service and its infrastructure. Do not publish a "no logs" statement unless your service actually follows and can substantiate a no-logs policy.

4. Server Configuration

ZVPN may retrieve active VPN server information from its backend infrastructure. This can include server name, country, city, host/address, port, protocol and availability status.

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

If Firebase services such as Firebase Firestore, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, or other third-party SDKs are enabled in the application, those services may process information according to their respective policies. The final published policy should list every third-party SDK actually included in the production build.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information handled by the application and its backend. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

ZVPN is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using ZVPN and uninstall the application at any time. Where applicable, Android permissions and system controls can also be managed through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, backend, or data practices change. The "Last updated" date will be changed when material revisions are published.
