User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Google Play Developer Data Safety and Privacy Standards

Overview

At VidSave, we believe in complete data transparency and user control. VidSave does not require an account and does not store your personal data on remote cloud servers.

Any data generated by your use of VidSave (such as recently pasted URLs, saved download records, and temporary cache) is stored strictly locally on your own device. You can permanently wipe this data at any moment.

Option 1: Clear In-App Download History (1 Tap)

To wipe your local URL and download history within the mobile app or web portal:

  1. Open VidSave.
  2. Navigate to the Download History section.
  3. Tap the "Clear History" button.
  4. Confirm the prompt. All stored history items are instantly and permanently deleted from local device storage.

Option 2: Clear Application Data via Android Settings

To erase 100% of temporary app cache, preferences, and local databases:

  1. Open your Android device’s Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps or Application Manager.
  3. Locate and tap on VidSave.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and Clear Cache.

Option 3: Clear Web Portal Cache & Storage

If you are using the online web downloader on our site:

  1. Tap the "Clear Stored History" button directly below the Downloader tool.
  2. Or open your browser settings > Clear Browsing Data > Select Cookies and site data.
