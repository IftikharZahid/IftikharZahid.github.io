Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026 • Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VidSave ("we," "our," or "the App"), developed and maintained by independent developer Iftikhar Zahid (iftikharzahid.me).

Your privacy is fundamental to our service. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we do and do not collect, how your data is handled when you use the VidSave mobile application and web utility, the permissions requested by the application, and your rights under global privacy frameworks including GDPR, CCPA, and the Google Play Developer Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

VidSave is built with a privacy-first, client-focused architecture. We do not require account registration, and we do not harvest personal data:

  • No Account Information: You do not need to register with a name, email address, password, or phone number to use VidSave.
  • No Social Media Logins: We never request or store your login credentials, passwords, or session tokens for any social media platform (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, etc.).
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not track, collect, or store your real-time GPS location, financial information, contacts, biometric data, or microphone audio.
  • No Cloud Storage of Your Media: Videos or audio tracks you download are transferred directly to your local device memory or browser storage. We do not maintain remote cloud repositories of your downloaded files.

3. Information We Process & How It Is Used

To deliver core functionality, VidSave processes limited technical inputs strictly for immediate operational needs:

  • Public Media URLs: When you paste a public URL into VidSave, our service parses the public web structure of the target link solely to locate the publicly available video/audio stream and return the media file to you. These URLs are processed ephemerally and are not tied to your personal identity.
  • Local Download History: The App maintains a local history record of your recently processed links on your device storage (via local SQLite or browser localStorage). This data resides entirely on your device and can be cleared at any time in one tap.
  • Standard Technical Diagnostics: Standard non-identifying diagnostics (such as device model, OS version, application version, crash logs, and network connection type) may be processed to troubleshoot performance issues and optimize compatibility.

4. Android Permissions & System Access

The VidSave Android application requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its utility operations:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to query the user-specified public video links and download media files over active Wi-Fi or cellular connections.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Used strictly to save downloaded video and audio files into your device’s accessible “Downloads” directory or gallery folder. VidSave does not read, access, or modify any unrelated personal media on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used solely to display real-time download progress and inform you when your video download is complete.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and app maintenance, the App may integrate reputable third-party SDKs that operate under their own privacy guidelines:

  • Google Play Services: Enables core Android platform components, updates, and licensing.
  • Google AdMob: May be utilized to serve non-intrusive banner or interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect pseudonymous advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to aggregate anonymous crash logs and bug reports to maintain app stability.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

VidSave does not target and is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at XahidCodes@gmail.com, and we will take prompt steps to remove any such information.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Under regulations including the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to:

  • Inquire about what data is processed.
  • Delete your local history by tapping “Clear History” within the app or clearing App Storage in your Android settings.
  • Revoke granted permissions (storage, notifications) at any time via Android System Settings.
  • Request assistance or data deletion inquiries by visiting our Data Deletion Instructions page.
