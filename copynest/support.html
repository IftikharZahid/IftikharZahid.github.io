How can we help?

For the fastest help, include your CopyNest version, Android version, device model, and a clear description of the issue. Do not email passwords or sensitive clipboard contents.

Clipboard behavior

Confirm that you are following the save workflow available in your installed version. Android may limit clipboard access depending on the app state and operating system.

Saved text is missing

Check the app’s available history and deletion controls. Do not clear app storage or uninstall until you understand whether that could remove locally stored data.

Privacy question

Read the Privacy Policy for data-handling details. The policy must be completed against the actual release before publication.
